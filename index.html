MediTrack Hospital ERP

One record, from the front desk to the pharmacy.

Waiting list, consultation, laboratory, nursing, dispensing and billing in a single patient record — running entirely on this computer.

  • The waiting list sets the order Reception decides who is next, so nobody can be skipped.
  • Vital signs are checked as you type Each reading is compared with the normal adult range straight away.
  • Alerts you can trust Only results and worsening patients interrupt you. Everything else is just logged.

Works without internet. No patient data leaves this computer.
